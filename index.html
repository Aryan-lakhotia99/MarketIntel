Algorithmic Indicators & Features

Monitored dynamically with real-time analytics engines.

Live Breakout Scanners

Monitors 52-week high breakouts, 52-week low breakdowns, and volume momentum spikes (2.5x volume average spikes) dynamically with millisecond updates.

Sector Money Heatmap

Visualizes institutional money rotation across index groups (Nifty IT, Nifty Bank, Nifty Auto, Nifty Metal, US Tech) based on volume weight shifts and price momentum grids.

Derivatives & Buildup

Tracks F&O market sentiment indicators including simulated Put-Call Ratio (PCR) speedometers and derivatives snapshots (Long Buildup, Short Covering, Short Buildup, Long Unwinding).
